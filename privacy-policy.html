Privacy Policy for Shah Surveyors ERP

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Shah Surveyors ERP (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an internal business management application used to support employee and business operations. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the application.

Important: This application is intended primarily for authorized users and organizational use. Access to features and information may depend on your role and permissions.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, mobile number, login credentials, and account/role information required to authenticate and manage access.
  • Employee and organizational information: information entered by authorized administrators or employees for employee management and business operations.
  • Attendance information: punch-in/punch-out records, attendance dates and times, and related verification information.
  • Location information: when attendance verification requires it, the application may collect device location at the time of attendance activity. Location is used for attendance verification and related organizational records.
  • Expense information: expense amounts, categories, descriptions, dates, approval status, and receipt images or documents submitted through the application.
  • Payroll and work information: salary/payroll and related employment information entered or managed by authorized users.
  • Task, survey, and equipment information: information created or managed through task management, survey tools, and equipment records.
  • Technical information: information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the application, such as device/app information and error or diagnostic information where available.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to authenticate users, provide application features, manage employees, record attendance, verify attendance where applicable, process expenses and receipts, manage payroll and tasks, conduct surveys, manage equipment, maintain records, communicate operational updates, improve reliability, prevent misuse, and protect the security of the application and organizational data.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized administrators, employees, or other authorized organizational users according to their assigned permissions. Information may also be processed by service providers that help us operate authentication, hosting, storage, analytics, security, or other application infrastructure, where applicable.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary for legitimate business, operational, legal, accounting, security, or compliance purposes, subject to applicable requirements and organizational policies.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your role and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. Requests should be made through your organization’s authorized administrator or the contact details provided by Shah Surveyors.

7. Children's Privacy

Shah Surveyors ERP is intended for business and organizational users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for independent use of the application.

8. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to provide functionality such as authentication, cloud storage, notifications, or other technical services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, business processes, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be made available through the same public policy page and will include a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns regarding Shah Surveyors ERP, please contact the Shah Surveyors administration using the official contact details provided by the organization or within the application.
